# CRO — Conversion Rate Optimization

Systematic diagnosis and fixing of conversion drop-off, not random redesign.

## Diagnose before prescribing

1. **Where exactly does drop-off happen** — top of funnel (never engage), mid-funnel (start but abandon a form), or at the final action (hesitate at payment/submit)? The fix is different for each.
2. **Friction vs. motivation problem** — is the user willing but blocked (confusing form, broken flow, too many required fields), or unwilling (unclear value prop, trust deficit, price objection)? Misdiagnosing this leads to fixing the wrong thing.

## High-leverage fixes, by drop-off point

**Top-of-funnel (landing page bounce)**
- Value proposition clear within 3 seconds of landing, above the fold, no scrolling required to understand what this is and why it matters.
- One primary call-to-action, visually dominant — competing CTAs split attention and reduce conversion on all of them.

**Mid-funnel (form/flow abandonment)**
- Reduce required fields to the functional minimum — every additional required field measurably increases abandonment.
- Progressive disclosure — ask for the minimum to start, defer optional/enrichment fields to after the core action.
- Visible progress indicators on multi-step flows — unclear-length forms feel longer and get abandoned more.

**Final-step (checkout/submit hesitation)**
- Trust signals near the commitment point — security badges, clear pricing with no surprise fees, visible support/contact option.
- Remove unnecessary account-creation requirements — forced signup before completing the primary action is a major conversion killer, especially for first-time users.

## Testing discipline

State the hypothesis before proposing the fix ("users are dropping at step 3 because the field labels are unclear, testing shorter/clearer labels should reduce this specific drop-off"), not just "let's redesign it." If A/B testing is available, test one variable at a time — simultaneous changes make it impossible to attribute the lift.

## Ghana/African-market note

Payment-method mismatch (card-only checkout in a Mobile Money-dominant market) is often the single largest CRO lever available — check this before optimizing button colors.
