# NDA Triage — Fast First-Pass NDA Screening

For quickly sorting incoming NDAs into "looks standard, low risk to route for quick sign-off" vs. "needs real legal review before anyone signs" — a triage tool, not a substitute for that review on anything material.

## Fast-check list, in priority order

1. **Mutual vs. one-way** — confirm it matches the actual relationship (if both sides will share confidential info, a one-way NDA protecting only the counterparty is a red flag worth immediately flagging back).
2. **Definition of "Confidential Information"** — overly broad definitions (e.g., covering information the receiving party already knew, or publicly available info) without standard carve-outs are worth flagging.
3. **Duration** — how long do confidentiality obligations last after the relationship ends? Indefinite/perpetual terms are unusual outside of specific contexts (trade secrets) and worth a second look for a standard business NDA.
4. **Non-solicit/non-compete riders** — some NDAs quietly bundle in non-solicitation of employees or customers — flag if present, since this is a materially different (and often more restrictive) commitment than pure confidentiality.
5. **Governing law/jurisdiction** — flag if it's a jurisdiction with meaningfully different legal norms than expected, especially relevant for cross-border deals.
6. **Injunctive relief / liquidated damages clauses** — flag unusually aggressive remedy language (automatic injunctive relief without needing to show harm, specific dollar liquidated damages) for legal review rather than routine sign-off.

## Triage output

A quick verdict: "Standard, low-risk — safe to route for signature" or "Contains [specific flagged item(s)] — route to counsel before signing," never a bare "looks fine" without naming what was actually checked.

## Boundary

This is a speed filter for routine NDAs, not a substitute for legal review on anything involving significant IP, unusual counterparties, or material business relationships — say so explicitly whenever escalation is warranted.
