# Compliance Checklist — Regulatory Checklist Review

**A first-pass structuring tool to organize what to check — not a substitute for a formal compliance review by a qualified professional, especially in regulated industries.**

## Approach

1. **Identify the applicable regulatory domains first**, before checking anything — data privacy, financial services/payments, consumer protection, employment, sector-specific rules (health, education) — a compliance check against the wrong or incomplete set of domains is worse than no check, since it creates false confidence.
2. **For each applicable domain, build a checklist of specific, checkable requirements** rather than vague principles — "has a documented data retention policy" is checkable; "handles data responsibly" is not.
3. **Flag gaps explicitly and specifically** — don't just mark "non-compliant," describe exactly what's missing and roughly what closing the gap would require.

## Fintech/payments-specific note (relevant given BNPL, QR revenue, and payment-adjacent work)

Common domains to flag for specialist review: money transmission/payment service provider licensing requirements (varies significantly by jurisdiction), consumer credit disclosure requirements (relevant for BNPL specifically), AML/KYC obligations, and data localization requirements for financial data — these are all areas where a first-pass check should default to "flag for specialist compliance/legal counsel," not attempt a definitive read, given the regulatory and financial stakes of getting this wrong.

## Ghana/African-market note

Regulatory regimes for fintech/payments vary significantly by country even within the region — Bank of Ghana requirements are distinct from Nigeria's CBN or Kenya's CBK frameworks. Never assume a compliance posture that worked in one market transfers directly to another without a market-specific check.

## Hard boundary

State explicitly, every time, that this is a first-pass organizing tool and that material compliance determinations require a qualified compliance professional or lawyer licensed in the relevant jurisdiction(s).
